Pro Calibration

🔬 Why calibration matters

The pro comparison baselines were originally hand-estimated from coaching research — not measured from actual video. That means your MediaPipe-measured angles are being compared against numbers from a different measurement system.

The calibration tool runs a pro player's real footage through the same MediaPipe pipeline used to analyze your skaters. Once calibrated, both sides of every comparison are measured identically — making scores and feedback significantly more accurate.
